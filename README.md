# AInKauf

Final project for the Building AI course

## Summary

A fully digitalized shopping process. On the one hand, the smart refrigerator can automatically detect which products need to be restocked. On the other hand, this information is integrated with offers from local supermarkets, ensuring that the best deals are purchased at the right time.



## Background

The AI-powered project would save time, effort, and money in everyday life. By automatically identifying products that need to be restocked and generating a shopping list, users would no longer need to plan their grocery shopping manually. In addition, the system would identify the best available offers from local supermarkets, helping consumers reduce their grocery expenses. Due to its practical application in everyday life, the solution has significant potential for widespread adoption across society, potentially becoming part of many consumers' daily routines.



## How is it used?

The system is accessed through a digital app on consumers' smartphones. The app is directly connected to the refrigerator's AI recognition system and can access all available offers from local supermarkets. It is also fully customizable, allowing users to enter their preferred meals and receive personalized suggestions for new recipes or optimized dishes that match their eating habits and preferences.




## Data sources and AI methods

The data comes from cameras inside the refrigerator. The AI uses images to identify the food and track the contents. The system collects new data automatically and stores it in an app. It can also use information provided by the user, such as food preferences.


## Challenges

The project cannot always recognize every food item correctly, especially when products are hidden or packaged. It also cannot guarantee that all supermarket prices and offers are up to date. Privacy is important because the system collects information about people's eating habits. Therefore, user data must be stored securely and used responsibly. In addition, the system should avoid unnecessary food purchases and reduce food waste.


## What next?

The project could grow by connecting the app to more supermarkets and adding personalized meal plans and automatic online shopping. To develop the project further, we would need skills in AI, software development, and business management. We would also need support from engineers, supermarkets, and potential investors. Finally, we would need user feedback to improve the system and make it more useful.
